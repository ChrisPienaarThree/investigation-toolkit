How your work is saved

  • Your work stays on your device, in this browser. It isn't sent to Istec Training or to anyone else. Another device or browser won't show it.
  • Save a copy at the end of every session. Use Save to file in the Casebook and Tripod Beta, or Download backup copy in the RCA Fault Tree. On an iPhone or iPad, the file goes to Files ▸ Downloads.
  • Safari may clear what a website keeps after about a week without a visit. Your saved file is your real copy. Clearing your browser history also removes your work from the browser.
  • To carry on from a saved file, open the tool and use Open file… (or Open case file… in Tripod Beta), then choose the file.
  • To hand work in, send the saved file, or the Word or Excel export, to your facilitator.

Tips for tablets and phones

  • Add the toolkit to your home screen so it opens like an app.
    • iPhone and iPad: in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
    • Android: in Chrome, tap ⋮, then Add to Home screen.
  • An iPad, or a phone turned sideways, gives the diagrams the room they need. Use two fingers to zoom and pan.
  • The draft-tree helper in Tripod Beta asks an AI assistant for a first draft. On your own device you can copy its prompt and paste the answer back.
